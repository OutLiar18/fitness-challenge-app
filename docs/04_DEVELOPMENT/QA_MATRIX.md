# Champions Legacy Challenge — QA Matrix

Last updated: 3 August 2026

## Core regression

Authentication, entries, Journal deletion, scoring, goals, progression, announcements, profile, administration, Rulebook, Points Guide and Legacy Coach.

## Season workflow

- Draft season validation and House count.
- Registration code and capacity.
- C.H.A.O.S. minimum roster, balance, one-time activation and private notifications.
- Direct route refresh on Seasons, Houses, Pocket and Notifications.
- Ballot open notification, one vote/member, 24-hour close, tie/no-vote resolution.
- Captain appointment of second Vice-Captain.
- Eligible roster swap, leader protection and weekly two-House lock.
- Contribution remains with old House after swap.
- Pocket date window, zero-point deposit, partial/whole redemption, empty balance and permanent receipt.
- Individual, House and honours calculations.

## Responsive checks

320, 375, 430, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1920 pixel widths; light/dark mode; keyboard-only; reduced motion; slow network; refresh on nested routes.
