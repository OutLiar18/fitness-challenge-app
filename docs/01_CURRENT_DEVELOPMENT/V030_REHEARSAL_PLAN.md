# Champions Legacy Challenge - v0.30.0 Complete League-Season Rehearsal Plan

Date: 12 August 2026
Status: Deferred until after v0.28.0 and v0.29.0
Origin: adapted from the earlier 28A rehearsal-planning work

## Purpose

v0.30.0 is the complete league-season rehearsal version.

It exists to prove that the app, after the page-inspection pass and friend-feedback pass,
can survive one coherent representative season from draft/registration through final
publication, honours and trusted recovery scenarios.

## Rehearsal coverage

The v0.30 rehearsal must cover the full lifecycle:

- draft and registration;
- C.H.A.O.S. opening House assignment;
- leadership;
- Pocket Week;
- active-season evidence and non-evidence activity flow;
- factual corrections;
- weekly roster movement and one-week stability;
- Power Plays;
- League Season bonus requests/awards/corrections;
- weekly House balancing;
- trusted reconciliation;
- publication and honours;
- trusted account deletion and recovery scenarios;
- adversarial Firestore Rules coverage where needed.

## Planned checkpoint structure

### 30A - rehearsal environment and canonical fixture
- establish the safe local/emulator rehearsal environment;
- define the canonical synthetic season and fixture rules.

### 30B - deterministic happy path
- prove one coherent season foundation from registration through baseline active weeks.

### 30C - adversarial weekly operations
- evidence, corrections, movement/stability, Power Plays, bonus administration and House balancing.

### 30D - trusted closeout and recovery
- reconciliation, publication, honours, deletion/recovery and final manual rehearsal.

### 30R - release freeze
- full regression, Rules gate, build verification and rehearsal acceptance closeout.

## Boundary

v0.30.0 should be started only after:
- v0.28.0 page inspection/change pass is complete; and
- v0.29.0 friend/external feedback changes are complete.
