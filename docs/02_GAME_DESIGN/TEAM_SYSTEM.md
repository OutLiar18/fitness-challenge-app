# Champions Legacy Challenge — Season House System

Last updated: 3 August 2026  
Implemented release: v0.14.0

## Purpose

Houses create themed identity, belonging, strategy and collective accountability inside one league season. They are not permanent profile Teams.

## Season scope

- Every House belongs to exactly one season.
- Players register for the season as individuals.
- C.H.A.O.S. creates the opening House rosters.
- A player has one current House within that season.
- The same player may belong to different Houses in different seasons.

## C.H.A.O.S.

C.H.A.O.S. means **Citizens Handpicked for Assignment via Operational Sorting**.

- Only a League or Platform Administrator may activate it.
- It may run once during Registration.
- All required Houses must exist.
- At least two registered players are required per House.
- The seeded shuffle is balanced so House sizes differ by no more than one.
- Each player receives a private assignment notification.

## Weekly leadership

- One 24-hour ballot per House per challenge week.
- Every current member has one private vote.
- Highest vote total becomes Captain.
- Second-highest becomes primary Vice-Captain.
- Ties, one-candidate outcomes and empty ballots require administrator resolution after the window closes.
- The Captain may appoint one additional Vice-Captain.
- A House has one Captain and no more than two Vice-Captains.

## Weekly roster movement

- Each House may participate in one balanced player swap each week.
- One eligible player leaves each House and one arrives.
- Captains, Vice-Captains and trusted league administrators may perform the action.
- A current leader must be replaced before moving.
- Both Houses are transactionally locked for that week after the swap.

## Historical points

Every contribution copies the player’s House at the time of earning. When a player moves:

- personal points remain with the player;
- previous House points remain with the previous House;
- future House points go to the new House.

Historical contribution documents are never rewritten to match current membership.

## Inactive mechanics

Diamonds, player prices, House Immunity, timed bidding, House votes on purchases and late-season twists remain inactive until separately designed.
