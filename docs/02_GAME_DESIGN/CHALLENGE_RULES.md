# Champions Legacy Challenge — Challenge Rules

Last updated: 3 August 2026

The player-facing source is the in-app Rulebook at `/rules`. It preserves the 2025 wording where accurate and labels every item as Current, Season or Inactive.

## v0.14 season rules

- Houses exist only inside one season.
- C.H.A.O.S. assigns the opening rosters once during Registration.
- At least two registered players are required per House.
- A 24-hour House leadership ballot may occur once per challenge week.
- Highest votes appoint the Captain; second-highest appoints the primary Vice-Captain.
- Administrator resolution is required when voting cannot produce both roles.
- Captains may appoint one additional Vice-Captain.
- Each House may participate in one balanced roster swap per week.
- Previous House contributions remain permanently allocated where earned.
- Pocket Week is the seven days immediately before the season begins.
- Pocket deposits earn zero points until activated by their owner during the Active season.
- Pocket balances are private, non-transferable and final after redemption.

## Inactive rules

Power Play, Diamonds, full Transfer Market, Buddy Bonus, photo bonuses, Five Fires and late-season twists award no points until explicitly activated in a future version.
