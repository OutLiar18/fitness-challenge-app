# Champions Legacy Challenge — Roadmap

Last updated: 1 August 2026

## Phase 1 — Core tracking platform

Status: Complete

- Authentication and profiles.
- Ten activity categories.
- Factual Firestore entries.
- Validation, normalisation and explainable Points Engine v2.
- Running/Cardio cross-contribution.
- Journal and local-safe dates.
- Libraries and suggestions.

## Phase 2 — Release confidence

Status: Ongoing discipline

Completed:

- Firestore rules deployment.
- Domain tests and safe audit review.
- Route-level lazy loading.
- Shared player-data provider.

Remaining:

- Error-monitoring strategy.
- Historical aggregation and pagination.
- Repeatable release QA automation.

## Phase 3 — Personal progression and experience

Status: Complete through v0.7.1

- Daily and weekly goals.
- Moderate completion bonuses.
- Forgiving streak and earned shield.
- XP, levels and titles.
- Achievements and personal records.
- Chronological progress timeline.
- Dedicated Progress and Profile routes.
- Adaptive desktop, tablet and mobile navigation.
- Dedicated Log & Journal workspace.
- Announcements foundation and motivational content.

## Phase 4 — Secure administration and moderation

Status: Next major phase

- Custom-claim or server-trusted administrator roles.
- Suggestion review queues.
- Announcement publishing.
- Library publishing tools.
- User and team management.
- Immutable audit history.
- Versioned challenge configuration.

No privileged action may rely only on a client-controlled profile field.

## Phase 5 — Competition and community

Status: Structured preview only

- Teams.
- Leagues and seasons.
- Leaderboards.
- Challenge templates.
- Social encouragement.

Competition must preserve consistency over natural athletic ability. League scoring must freeze an immutable versioned ruleset.

## Phase 6 — Intelligence and analytics

Status: Structured preview only

- Trend charts.
- Weekly summaries.
- Goal recommendations.
- Training and habit insights.
- Transparent, user-controlled Legacy Coach.

## Guardrails

- Never duplicate scoring or progression logic in UI components.
- Never simulate unfinished security-sensitive functionality.
- Store facts; derive progress.
- Humour and easter eggs must remain optional, respectful and non-blocking.
- Do not start leagues before secure administration and immutable ruleset snapshots.
