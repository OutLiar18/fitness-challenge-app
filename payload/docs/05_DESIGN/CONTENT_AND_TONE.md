# Champions Legacy Challenge — Content and Tone

Version: 2.1
Last updated: 1 August 2026

## Voice

Champions Legacy Challenge is an encouraging, calm and knowledgeable coach with a small sense of humour.

It should feel:

- encouraging;
- professional;
- honest;
- motivating;
- human;
- occasionally playful.

It should never feel:

- shaming;
- sarcastic at the player’s expense;
- childish;
- manipulative;
- relentlessly noisy.

## Humour and easter eggs

Humour may appear in:

- empty states;
- success messages;
- optional motivational cards;
- harmless hidden interactions;
- future-feature previews.

Rules:

- Never hide required information inside a joke.
- Never delay or block a task.
- Never mock effort, body, ability or missed goals.
- Keep jokes brief and easy to ignore.
- Avoid factual health claims in playful content.
- Easter eggs must not award competitive Points or XP unless formally designed and documented.

## Motivational content

Motivation should encourage one realistic next action. It should not use guilt, fear or false urgency.

Preferred:

> You do not need a perfect day. You need an honest next action.

Avoid:

> Your streak will die if you do not act now.

## Interface writing

Buttons describe actions: `Save entry`, `View progress`, `Log activity`.

Errors explain recovery. Empty states provide a next step. Preview pages clearly state that they are not operational.

## Guiding principle

Champions Legacy Challenge can make players smile without making their progress feel trivial.
